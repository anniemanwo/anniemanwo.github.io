What I bring

Creative instincts.
Commercial thinking.

I’m Mariana. I like finding the simple idea inside a complicated brief, then giving it a voice, a structure, and a visual world people can remember.

My background in marketing, customer experience, and sales shapes how I design: I listen closely, ask practical questions, and keep the audience at the center. I know how to plan, create, and design responsive webpages—from organizing the content and user flow to shaping the visual style and final experience. I also use AI for research, creative ideation, visual exploration, and rapid prototyping, always refining the result with my own judgment so the work stays thoughtful, distinctive, and genuinely useful.
